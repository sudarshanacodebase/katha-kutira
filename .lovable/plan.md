# Katha Kutira homepage

## Goal
Create a warm, modern audio-first homepage that introduces Katha Kutira as a welcoming home for Indian children’s stories.

## What I’ll build
- A responsive homepage with the supplied palette and Poppins typography.
- A story-cottage hero featuring “Every Story Has a Home.” and both requested actions.
- Featured story cards with cover art, metadata, favourites, and playable audio controls.
- Search and discovery filters for age, language, duration, category, and mood.
- Category browsing across all ten requested story collections.
- The three-step listening journey, product benefits, regional storytelling, storytellers, parent testimonials marked as placeholders, FAQs, newsletter signup, and full footer.
- A persistent mini audio player that works across the page.
- Accessible interactions, responsive mobile navigation, and page-specific search metadata.

## Visual direction
- Warm terracotta accents over a quiet blue-grey background.
- Storybook-inspired cottage, tree, animal, lamp, and star imagery with restrained decorative motifs.
- Premium editorial spacing, compact rounded corners, and playful details without becoming cartoon-heavy or religious.

## Technical details
- Keep all palette and typography choices as reusable design tokens.
- Model story content as structured reusable data and components.
- Use generated local artwork rather than external image links or placeholders.
- Keep this first version frontend-only; favourites, filters, search, and playback work in the current session without accounts or saved cloud data.
